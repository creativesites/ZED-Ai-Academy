# Glory Academy — Initial Beginner AI Courses (plan)

Five practical, beginner-level AI courses to launch Glory Academy on CS Learn. Each course is built from the block types the course builder already has: video, text, steps, ai-prompt, practice-exercise, before-after, case-study, checklist, comparison-table, tool-spotlight, glossary, key-takeaway, assignment, tutor-session (AI teacher), and a quiz at the end of every module.

## Design rules for all five

- **Audience:** Zambian adults with a smartphone and no technical background: workers, traders, job seekers, students, teachers.
- **Mobile- and data-first:** lessons of 6–10 minutes. Text and steps come first and video is optional (short YouTube clips of 3 minutes or less). Every exercise works on a phone with free AI apps.
- **Free tools only:** ChatGPT, Google Gemini and Microsoft Copilot (free tiers), Google Lens / Translate, Canva (free). Don't teach anything that needs a paid plan.
- **Local examples:** kwacha amounts, ZESCO, mobile money, markets like Soweto and Kamwala, ZRA, ECZ exams, Zambian CVs and job boards. Use local names in scenarios.
- **Learn by doing:** every lesson ends with a "Try it now" ai-prompt or practice-exercise block. Every course ends with a small real-world project (assignment block).
- **Safety is built in:** each course covers what not to paste into AI (NRC numbers, mobile-money PINs, client data), checking facts, and honest use.
- **AI teacher:** one tutor-session block per course, used for spoken practice or a guided check. The course certificate can use the AI viva (`require_certificate_viva`) for courses 1 and 4.
- **Structure:** 4 modules × 3 lessons + a module quiz (5 questions) is roughly 2–2.5 hours per course.

## Course 1 — AI Basics: Your First Steps with AI

**Promise:** In two hours you'll understand what AI is, have a working AI assistant on your phone, and be able to ask it for useful help safely.
**Outcomes:** explain AI in plain words · set up a free assistant · write clear requests · spot wrong answers · keep personal data safe.

| Module | Lessons (blocks) |
|---|---|
| **1. What AI really is** | 1.1 AI in plain language: what it can and can't do (text, glossary) · 1.2 Where you already use AI: maps, spam filters, translation (case-study) · 1.3 Myths vs facts: "AI knows everything", "AI will take every job" (comparison-table, key-takeaway) |
| **2. Getting set up** | 2.1 Choosing a free assistant: ChatGPT vs Gemini vs Copilot (comparison-table, tool-spotlight) · 2.2 Installing and signing in on your phone, step by step (steps, checklist) · 2.3 Your first conversation (ai-prompt: "Explain how ZESCO units work in simple English") |
| **3. Asking good questions** | 3.1 The 4 ingredients: who you are, what you want, details, format (steps, before-after) · 3.2 Follow-up questions: make it shorter, simpler, in a table (practice-exercise) · 3.3 Asking for help in English, Nyanja or Bemba and checking the result (ai-prompt) |
| **4. Using AI safely and wisely** | 4.1 When AI is wrong: hallucinations and how to double-check (before-after, checklist) · 4.2 What never to share: PINs, NRC, passwords, other people's data (checklist, risk-assessment) · 4.3 Scams that use AI: fake voices, fake messages (case-study, key-takeaway) |

**AI teacher session:** "Explain AI to a friend": the learner explains AI back in their own words and the teacher gives gentle feedback.
**Project:** use AI to plan one real task (a weekly budget, a shopping list or a letter) and submit the before/after.
**Certificate:** short AI viva: "Name two things AI is good at and one risk."

## Course 2 — AI on Your Phone: Everyday Help in Minutes

**Promise:** Use the phone you already own to translate, read documents, write messages and solve everyday problems with AI.
**Outcomes:** use voice with AI · translate and read signs and documents with the camera · draft messages and letters · plan money and time · get study and health information responsibly.

| Module | Lessons (blocks) |
|---|---|
| **1. Talk to your phone** | 1.1 Voice mode: speaking instead of typing (steps, tool-spotlight) · 1.2 Turning a voice note into neat written notes (practice-exercise) · 1.3 Which AI tools are already on your phone (checklist; tools vary by phone and region) |
| **2. See with your camera** | 2.1 Google Lens: translate a sign, a label or a form (steps) · 2.2 Photograph a letter or bill and ask AI to explain it (ai-prompt using a sample ZESCO or water bill) · 2.3 Scanning handwritten notes into text (practice-exercise) |
| **3. Write anything faster** | 3.1 Polite messages to a landlord, school or employer (before-after) · 3.2 Formal letters and complaints (ai-prompt, template) · 3.3 Translating messages for family: English ↔ Nyanja and others (ai-prompt + "check with a native speaker" callout) |
| **4. Plan your life** | 4.1 Monthly budget in kwacha with AI (practice-exercise, table output) · 4.2 Meal plans, schedules and to-do lists (ai-prompt) · 4.3 Health and legal questions: when to trust AI and when to see a professional (expert-note, checklist) |

**AI teacher session:** role-play writing a WhatsApp message to resolve a problem with a supplier or landlord.
**Project:** make your own personal "AI cheat sheet" of five prompts you'll reuse every week.

## Course 3 — AI for Small Business & Side Hustles

**Promise:** Use free AI tools to market, sell and run a small business, with a full week of social posts ready by the end.
**Outcomes:** write product descriptions and posts · make simple designs · reply to customers professionally · price products and track sales · write a one-page business plan.

| Module | Lessons (blocks) |
|---|---|
| **1. Know your customer** | 1.1 Describe your business to AI and get customer profiles (ai-prompt) · 1.2 Market research without money: asking AI the right questions and checking them on the ground (steps, checklist) · 1.3 Case study: a Soweto Market vegetable seller (case-study) |
| **2. Marketing that sells** | 2.1 Product descriptions and prices that convert (before-after) · 2.2 A week of Facebook / WhatsApp Status posts in 10 minutes (practice-exercise) · 2.3 Simple flyers and posts with Canva's free AI (tool-spotlight, steps) |
| **3. Serving customers** | 3.1 Professional replies to enquiries and complaints (ai-prompt, before-after) · 3.2 FAQ and price list for WhatsApp Business (practice-exercise) · 3.3 Handling customer data responsibly (checklist) |
| **4. Running the numbers** | 4.1 Costing and pricing a product with AI as calculator and coach (practice-exercise, comparison-table) · 4.2 A simple sales and stock tracker in Google Sheets with AI-written formulas (steps) · 4.3 One-page business plan and a loan / grant pitch (ai-prompt, assignment template) |

**AI teacher session:** "Pitch your business in 60 seconds", with spoken feedback on clarity and confidence.
**Project:** submit a one-page plan plus 7 ready-to-post marketing messages and one designed flyer.

## Course 4 — Get Hired with AI: CVs, Applications & Interviews

**Promise:** Build a strong CV and cover letter, find and apply for jobs smarter, and practise interviews out loud with an AI teacher.
**Outcomes:** tailor a CV to a job advert · write a focused cover letter · improve a LinkedIn profile · prepare STAR answers · practise and get feedback on interviews · use AI honestly.

| Module | Lessons (blocks) |
|---|---|
| **1. Your story** | 1.1 List your skills and experience with AI's help: AI interviews you (ai-prompt) · 1.2 Turning duties into achievements (before-after) · 1.3 What recruiters in Zambia look for (expert-note, checklist) |
| **2. CV and cover letter** | 2.1 A clean one-to-two-page CV, step by step (steps, template) · 2.2 Tailoring your CV to a specific job advert (practice-exercise with a sample advert) · 2.3 A cover letter that isn't generic (before-after, ai-prompt) |
| **3. Finding and applying** | 3.1 Upgrading your LinkedIn profile (steps) · 3.2 Searching smarter and spotting fake job adverts and recruitment scams (case-study, checklist) · 3.3 Tracking applications and following up politely (practice-exercise) |
| **4. Interview ready** | 4.1 Common questions and STAR answers (steps, ai-prompt) · 4.2 Practise out loud with the AI teacher (tutor-session) · 4.3 Honesty: why you should never fake a CV or use AI during a live test (key-takeaway, discussion-prompt) |

**AI teacher session (core):** mock interview for the learner's target role, followed by a feedback report.
**Project:** submit a tailored CV and cover letter for a real advert.
**Certificate:** AI viva, a 3-question mini-interview.

## Course 5 — Learning & Teaching with AI

**Promise:** Students learn how to study faster with AI without cheating. Teachers learn how to prepare lessons, tests and feedback in a fraction of the time.
**Outcomes:** use AI as a tutor, not a ghost-writer · make revision notes, flashcards and practice questions · (teachers) build lesson plans, worksheets and marking rubrics · understand school rules and academic honesty.

| Module | Lessons (blocks) |
|---|---|
| **1. AI as your study partner** | 1.1 Tutor or cheat? Using AI the right way (comparison-table, key-takeaway) · 1.2 "Explain it like I'm 12", then harder (ai-prompt) · 1.3 Checking AI's answers against your textbook and the ECZ syllabus (checklist) |
| **2. Revise smarter** | 2.1 Summaries and revision notes from your own notes (practice-exercise) · 2.2 Flashcards and self-quizzes (ai-prompt) · 2.3 A study timetable before exams (steps, template) |
| **3. For teachers: plan faster** | 3.1 Lesson plans aligned to the syllabus topic (ai-prompt, template) · 3.2 Worksheets at three difficulty levels (practice-exercise) · 3.3 Making tests and marking guides, and checking them (steps, checklist) |
| **4. Fair, safe and effective** | 4.1 Writing honest feedback faster (before-after) · 4.2 Spotting AI-written homework without false accusations (case-study, expert-note) · 4.3 Protecting pupils' data (checklist, risk-assessment) |

**AI teacher session:** "Teach it back". The learner explains a topic they just studied, and the teacher asks follow-up questions and scores understanding.
**Project:** students submit a one-week AI study plan with sample flashcards. Teachers submit one full lesson pack (plan, worksheet, quiz and rubric).

## Build checklist (per course)

1. Create the course in Glory Academy as a **draft**: category "AI Skills", level `beginner`, thumbnail in brand colours.
2. Use the AI blueprint generator to draft modules and lessons from this outline, then edit every lesson for local examples and accuracy.
3. Add one "Try it now" block per lesson and a 5-question quiz per module.
4. Configure the tutor-session scenario (persona, goal, rubric).
5. Test on a mid-range Android phone on mobile data: each lesson should load quickly and every exercise should work with free tools.
6. Publish course 1 first (free), then add one course every week or two and promote each one to course 1's graduates.
